ACCOUNT SCOPE

Taiwan ETF tracking

Record the actual transactions appearing in the Cathay Securities account. ETF reserve cash is an emergency/backup fund, separate from Cash Savings and CFD funds. Normal ETF purchases are funded externally and normal sale proceeds leave to an external account; either trade may use ETF reserve cash only when its special route is selected.
