Statements & Net Worth

Inventory counts and statement categories

For inventory sales, enter total stock at cost on the day before the reporting period and on the closing date. A confirmed empty inventory has a value of zero. The dated counts cover the whole budget in its reporting currency.

Use Income → Other with “Inventory sales”, “Service sales”, or “Capital introduced”. For purchases or drawings use Expense → General → Other with “Inventory purchases” or “Drawings”. Collections and loan repayments use their existing linked forms.

Backup & Local Storage

Budget records are saved locally; the exchange-rate service receives currency requests only. This app stores your records, including small optional receipt attachments, in this browser only. Closing the tab does not erase saved records, but clearing site data, using private mode, changing the file origin, or reaching your browser storage limit may. Keep full JSON backups outside your browser.
