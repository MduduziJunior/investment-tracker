Backup & Local Storage

Budget records are saved locally; the exchange-rate service receives currency requests only. This app stores your records, including small optional receipt attachments, in this browser only. Closing the tab does not erase saved records, but clearing site data, using private mode, changing the file origin, or reaching your browser storage limit may. Keep full JSON backups outside your browser.
